Painting work

We specialize in quality workmanship on interior and exterior painting projects on all residential and commercial work. We understand that we are guests in your house. The utmost respect is paid to maintaining an environment that is as safe and clean as possible.

PREPARATION

  • We use clean plastic drop sheets on all furniture.
  • Durable canvas drop sheets cover the floor.
  • During sanding, plastic sheets will be taped over entrances to contain the dust.
  • All holes and cracks are filled and feather sanded to ensure all edges are smoothed.

FINISH

  • Top grade paints are applied to ensure quality and a job that is washable and durable.
  • All cut lines and edging are neat and clean.
