Electrical work

Electrical work means:

connecting electricity supply wiring to electrical equipment or disconnecting electricity supply wiring from electrical equipment; or manufacturing, constructing, installing, removing, adding, testing, replacing, repairing, altering or maintaining electrical equipment or an electrical installation.

Examples of electrical work:

Installing low voltage electrical wiring in a building Installing electrical equipment into an installation coupler or interconnecter Replacing a low voltage electrical component of a washing machine Maintaining an electricity entity’s overhead distribution system.
